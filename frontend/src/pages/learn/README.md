# frontend/src/pages/learn

The learning screens: the path, a module, a lesson, the module quest and the learner's classes.

## Files in this folder

- **LearnLayout.jsx** (Member 2) - One screen of the website.
- **LessonView.jsx** (Member 2) - One lesson: the story screen, the engine, and what finishing means.
- **ModulePage.jsx** (Member 2) - One module: its words and its four lessons.
- **ModuleQuest.jsx** (Member 2) - The end-of-module test.
- **MyClasses.jsx** (Member 3) - A learner's classes: join by code, find a class, live link, homework and materials.
- **PathPage.jsx** (Member 2) - The trail of chapters and modules for one track.
